# Automation Priority Guide

**Purpose**: Systematically automate your agency to work 20 hours/week while scaling to £1M/month.

**Philosophy**: Automate the repetitive, preserve the relationship. Machines for tasks, humans for thinking.

---

## 🎯 The Automation Strategy

**Goal**: Automate 80% of tasks, spend human time on the 20% that matters most (strategy, relationships, creative thinking).

**Principle**: Automate in order of:
1. **Highest time cost** (What takes the most hours?)
2. **Lowest value-add** (What doesn't require human judgment?)
3. **Most error-prone** (What do humans forget/mess up?)

## 🚨 Automation Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Don't Do This:
1. Automate before documenting the process (automate bad process = bad automation)
2. Over-automate relationships (clients want humans, not bots)
3. Set and forget (automation needs monitoring)
4. Make it too complex (simple automation = reliable automation)
5. Automate before you understand it manually

### ✅ Do This Instead:
1. Document → Manual → Automate → Monitor
2. Automate tasks, preserve relationships
3. Review automation weekly
4. Keep it simple and clear
5. Master the process, then automate it
