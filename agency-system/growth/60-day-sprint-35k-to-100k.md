## 📋 The Specific Tactics That Will Work

### Tactic 1: The "Fast Decision" Offer

**Create urgency**:
- "If you sign by [date], we'll include [bonus worth £X]"
- "We have 2 slots opening up this month"
- "First month at 20% off if you start by [date]"

**Make it easy to say yes**:
- Monthly contracts (not annual) to reduce risk
- Fast start date (within 1 week)
- Clear deliverables and timeline
- Money-back guarantee if needed

---

### Tactic 2: The Upsell Conversation

**Script for existing clients**:
```
"[Client], I've been looking at your results - you're doing great with [current service].

I've identified an opportunity: if we add [new service], we could [specific result]. Based on what I'm seeing, this could generate an additional [£X or X%] for you.

The investment would be [£Y/month], and based on our projections, you'd see [ROI] within [timeframe].

Would you like me to put together a detailed plan?"
```

**What to upsell**:
- If they have ads → add landing page optimization
- If they have SEO → add content marketing
- If they have content → add paid promotion
- If they have social → add paid social ads
- If they have one channel → add second channel

**Target**: 50-100% increase per client
- £8k client → £12-15k
- £10k client → £15-18k

---

### Tactic 3: The Price Increase

**When client renews**:
```
"[Client], we're coming up on your renewal in [X weeks].

First, I want to thank you - working together has been fantastic, and I'm proud of what we've achieved: [list results].

For the next phase, I'm recommending we continue at [new price] per month. This reflects [reason: market rates increased / expanded scope / additional value].

The new agreement would start on [date]. Any questions?"
```

**Justifications**:
- Market rates have increased
- Your costs have increased
- You've expanded scope
- Results justify higher investment
- Additional value you're providing

**Target**: 10-20% increase
- £10k → £11-12k
- £15k → £16.5-18k

---

**Daily outbound target**: 20-30 touches
- 10 emails

**Who to target**:

### Tactic 5: The Partnership Play

**Find complementary agencies**:
- They do web design, you do marketing
- They do branding, you do campaigns
- They do PR, you do digital

**Partnership pitch**:
```
"Hey [Agency Owner], I run a [X] agency helping [clients]. I noticed you do [Y].

I have clients who need [Y], and I bet you have clients who need [X].

What if we referred clients to each other? I'd send you [X type] clients, you send me [Y type] clients. No fees, just mutual value.

Interested?"
```

**Result**: Instant warm lead pipeline

## 🚨 What Will Try to Stop You

### Obstacle 1: "I don't have capacity"

**Solution**:
- Hire NOW (don't wait)
- Use contractors/freelancers for overflow
- Use Claude Code to 2x productivity
- Reduce scope on some clients temporarily
- Raise prices to reduce volume needs

### Obstacle 2: "Sales take too long"

**Solution**:
- Shorter proposals (2 pages max)
- Same-day turnaround on proposals
- Fast decision incentive ("Sign this week and...")
- Smaller initial scope (easier yes)
- Start fast (within 1 week)

### Obstacle 3: "Quality will suffer"

**Solution**:
- Use your quality control system
- Hire before you're desperate
- Use SOPs and templates
- Over-communicate with clients
- Under-promise, over-deliver

### Obstacle 4: "Clients won't pay more"

**Solution**:
- Stop working with cheap clients
- Target companies with budgets
- Show ROI clearly
- Charge for value, not hours
- Confidence in pricing
