## 📊 Key Metrics Dashboard

### Growth Metrics
- **MRR**: Monthly recurring revenue
- **MRR Growth Rate**: Target 8-15% per month early stage, 4-8% at scale
- **ARR**: Annual recurring revenue (MRR × 12)
- **New MRR**: From new clients this month
- **Expansion MRR**: Upsells from existing clients
- **Churned MRR**: Lost from clients leaving
- **Net New MRR**: New + Expansion - Churned

### Client Metrics
- **Number of active clients**: Track total and by tier
- **Average client value**: MRR ÷ number of clients
- **Client retention rate**: % staying month-to-month (target: 95%+)
- **Client lifetime**: Average months they stay
- **Client LTV**: Average revenue over lifetime
- **NPS**: Net Promoter Score (target: 50+)
- **Client health score**: Average across all clients (target: 4.0+)

### Sales Metrics
- **Qualified leads per month**: Target 20-50 at scale
- **Lead-to-close rate**: % of leads that become clients (target: 25-30%)
- **Sales cycle length**: Days from first contact to signed (target: 30-60)
- **CAC**: Customer acquisition cost (target: <20% of LTV)
- **LTV:CAC ratio**: Lifetime value ÷ CAC (target: 3:1+)

### Financial Metrics
- **Gross margin**: (Revenue - Direct costs) ÷ Revenue (target: 60-65%)
- **EBITDA margin**: Operating profit ÷ Revenue (target: 20-25%)
- **Revenue per employee**: MRR ÷ number of employees (target: £20-25k)
- **Cash runway**: Months of expenses in the bank (target: 6+ months)
- **Days sales outstanding (DSO)**: Average days to collect payment (target: <30)

### Operational Metrics
- **Team utilization**: % of time on billable work (target: 70-75%)
- **On-time delivery rate**: % of deliverables delivered on time (target: 95%+)
- **Project profitability**: Revenue per project - costs (track and optimize)
- **Employee retention**: % of team staying year-over-year (target: 85%+)

## 💡 Lessons from £1M Agencies

### What They Did Right
- **Niched down early**: "We do X for Y" not "we do everything"
- **Built systems**: Process for everything
- **Hired slow**: Took time to find right people
- **Focused on retention**: Kept clients 2+ years average
- **Premium positioning**: Not the cheapest, but the best
- **Thought leadership**: Built brand through content
- **Strong culture**: Team wanted to stay and perform

### What They Wish They'd Done Differently
- **Hired senior talent earlier**: Waited too long to get experienced leaders
- **Said no more**: Took on wrong clients that caused problems
- **Raised prices sooner**: Left money on the table
- **Systematized earlier**: Spent too long doing everything manually
- **Invested in tools**: Penny-wise, pound-foolish on software
- **Focused more**: Tried to do too many services
