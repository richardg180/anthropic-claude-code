## 📊 Growth Framework

### The 4 Growth Levers

1. **Client Acquisition**: Get more clients
   - Outbound, inbound, referrals
   - Target: 2-4 new clients per month at scale

2. **Client Retention**: Keep clients longer
   - Use your client success system
   - Target: 95%+ retention rate

3. **Average Client Value**: Grow revenue per client
   - Upselling, price increases, scope expansion
   - Target: 10-15% annual growth

4. **Operational Efficiency**: Do more with less
   - Better systems, automation, team leverage
   - Target: £20-25k MRR per employee

## 📈 Key Metrics to Track

### Growth Metrics
- MRR and growth rate
- New MRR vs Churned MRR
- Average client value
- Number of clients

### Sales Metrics
- Qualified leads per month
- Close rate (target: 25-30%)
- Sales cycle (target: 30-60 days)
- CAC and LTV:CAC ratio (target: 3:1+)

### Financial Metrics
- Gross margin (target: 60-65%)

- Revenue per employee (target: £20-25k)

### Client Metrics
- Retention rate (target: 95%+)
- NPS (target: 50+)
- Average client health score (target: 4.0+)

## 💡 Growth Best Practices

### DO ✅
- Focus on one phase at a time
- Measure everything
- Invest in systems early
- Hire for the next stage, not current
- Keep retention above 95%
- Build referral engine
- Premium positioning
- Say no to distractions

### DON'T ❌
- Try to skip phases
- Grow without systems
- Hire too fast (or too slow)
- Sacrifice quality for growth
- Compete on price
- Take on wrong-fit clients
- Try to do everything yourself
- Ignore the metrics
