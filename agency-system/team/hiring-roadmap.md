# Hiring Roadmap to £1M per Month

**Purpose**: Know exactly who to hire, when to hire them, and what they should do at every stage.

**Principle**: Hire for the next stage, not your current stage. Hire when pain > cost.

---

## 📊 Team Size at Each Revenue Milestone

| Revenue (MRR) | Team Size | Revenue per Employee | Key Hires This Stage |
|---------------|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| £0-50k | 1-3 people | £25k+ | You + 1-2 delivery specialists |
| £50-150k | 3-7 people | £20k+ | Account Manager, 2-3 specialists, VA |
| £150-300k | 10-15 people | £20k+ | Account Director, Ops Manager, more specialists |
| £300-500k | 20-25 people | £18k+ | Head of Sales, senior specialists, sales team |
| £500-750k | 28-35 people | £20k+ | COO, Head of Delivery, team leads |
| £750k-1M | 35-40 people | £25k+ | Full C-suite, optimize not add |

**Target at £1M/month**: 35-40 people = £25k+ revenue per employee

## 🎯 The Complete Hiring Journey

### Phase 1: £0-50k MRR (Just You or 1-3 People)

**Timeline**: Months 0-6

**Your Team**:
1. **You** - Everything (CEO, salesperson, account manager, strategist)
2. **Hire #1**: Delivery Specialist in your core service (£30-45k)
   - *Hire when*: You're turning down work or working 60+ hours
   - *They do*: Execute the core work (ads management, content, design)
3. **Hire #2** (optional): Second specialist or VA (£25-35k or £15-25k)

**What You Do**: Sales, strategy, client relationships, everything they can't do

**Hours You Work**: 50-60/week

**When to Move to Next Phase**: Hitting £50k MRR consistently for 2-3 months

## 🎯 Critical Hiring Milestones

### The 5 Most Important Hires (In Order)

**1. First Delivery Specialist (£0-50k MRR)**
- *Why critical*: Gets you out of execution, lets you sell more
- *When*: As soon as you have 2-3 clients or working 60+ hours
- *Impact*: 2x your capacity

**2. Account Manager (£50-150k MRR)**
- *Why critical*: Takes client communication off your plate
- *When*: When managing 5+ clients becomes overwhelming
- *Impact*: 3x your capacity, improves client satisfaction

**3. Account Director (£150-300k MRR)**
- *Why critical*: First level of management, multiplies your leadership
- *When*: When you have 2-3 Account Managers to oversee
- *Impact*: You step out of client management, focus on growth

**4. Operations Manager (£150-300k MRR)**
- *Why critical*: Takes delivery management off your plate
- *When*: When coordinating 5+ specialists becomes chaos
- *Impact*: Quality improves, you focus on strategy

**5. COO (£500-750k MRR)**
- *Why critical*: Runs entire company operations so you can be CEO
- *When*: When executive team needs coordination
- *Impact*: You work 20 hours/week, company runs without you daily

## 💰 Hiring Budget at Each Stage

### Salary Budget as % of Revenue

**£0-150k MRR**: 35-45% of revenue on salaries
- Lower margins, still learning efficiency

**£150-500k MRR**: 40-50% of revenue on salaries
- Investing in team to scale

**£500k-1M MRR**: 35-45% of revenue on salaries
- Improving efficiency, better margins

**Example at £1M MRR**:
- Revenue: £1M/month = £12M/year
- Salary budget: 40% = £4.8M/year
- Average salary: £4.8M ÷ 40 people = £120k per person
- (Includes employer costs, not just base salary)

## 📋 Your Hiring Checklist at Each Stage

### When to Hire Your Next Person

**Signs You Need to Hire**:
- [ ] You or team working 50+ hour weeks consistently
- [ ] Turning down work due to capacity
- [ ] Quality declining due to overload
- [ ] You're doing work below your pay grade
- [ ] Client satisfaction scores dropping
- [ ] You haven't had a vacation in 6+ months

**Before You Hire**:
- [ ] Can we automate this work instead?
- [ ] Can we outsource temporarily (freelancer/contractor)?
- [ ] Do we have consistent revenue to support this salary?
- [ ] Do we have 6+ months runway for this role?
- [ ] Have we documented what this role will do?
- [ ] Do we have someone to manage this person?

**After You Hire**:
- [ ] Onboard them properly (first week planned)
- [ ] Give them clear goals and KPIs
- [ ] Check in daily first week, weekly first month
- [ ] Review performance at 30, 60, 90 days

## 💡 Hiring Best Practices

### DO ✅
- Hire for next stage, not current stage
- Hire slow (take your time finding right person)
- Check references thoroughly
- Define role clearly before hiring
- Hire for culture fit and aptitude, train for skills
- Invest in onboarding (first 90 days critical)
- Have 6+ months runway before hiring

### DON'T ❌
- Hire your friends (usually doesn't work)
- Hire too fast out of desperation
- Hire for current problem only
- Skip the probation period evaluation
- Hire mini-yous (need complementary skills)
- Forget to fire fast if it's not working
- Hire without capacity to manage them
