# Client Renewal Management System

**Purpose**: Ensure maximum client retention through proactive renewal management

**Target Renewal Rate**: 90%+

---

## 🎯 Renewal Philosophy

**Key Principle**: Renewals are won every day through exceptional work, not just during contract negotiations.

The renewal process starts the day a client signs, not 30 days before the contract ends.

---

## 📅 Renewal Timeline & Playbook

### Day 1: Contract Starts
- [ ] Add renewal date to calendar (set reminder 90 days before)
- [ ] Document client's stated goals and success criteria
- [ ] Set up tracking for KPIs that matter to them
- [ ] Plan first 90 days for quick wins

### Ongoing: Throughout Contract
- [ ] Deliver exceptional results consistently
- [ ] Communicate proactively (don't make them chase you)
- [ ] Document all wins and results
- [ ] Address issues immediately
- [ ] Build strong personal relationships
- [ ] Provide value beyond the scope when possible
- [ ] Monthly check-ins to ensure satisfaction

### 90 Days Before Renewal
- [ ] Internal renewal strategy meeting
- [ ] Review client health score (should be 4+)
- [ ] Compile results and ROI data
- [ ] Identify upsell opportunities
- [ ] Draft renewal proposal
- [ ] Schedule informal check-in with client

**90-Day Renewal Prep Checklist**:
- [ ] Client health score reviewed
- [ ] All KPIs documented with results
- [ ] ROI calculated and documented
- [ ] Case study or testimonial requested (if thriving)
- [ ] Upsell opportunities identified
- [ ] Competitive landscape reviewed
- [ ] Pricing strategy determined
- [ ] Renewal proposal drafted
- [ ] Internal team aligned on approach

### 60 Days Before Renewal
- [ ] Casual conversation: "Just checking in on goals for next phase"
- [ ] Ask: "What would make this partnership even more valuable?"
- [ ] Listen for any concerns or hesitations
- [ ] Plant seeds for upsell if appropriate
- [ ] Ensure deliverables are on track

**Questions to Ask at 60 Days**:
- "How are you feeling about our partnership?"
- "Are we meeting your expectations?"
- "What could we do better?"
- "What are your goals for the next 6-12 months?"
- "Are there any other areas where we could help?"

### 45 Days Before Renewal
- [ ] Prepare comprehensive results presentation
- [ ] Build renewal business case
- [ ] Finalize pricing and proposal
- [ ] Address any concerns surfaced at 60-day check
- [ ] Ensure client is seeing strong results

**Results Presentation Should Include**:
- Goal vs. actual performance for all KPIs
- Clear ROI calculation
- Wins and achievements timeline
- Comparison to benchmarks/industry averages
- Future opportunities and recommendations
- Next phase strategy preview

### 30 Days Before Renewal
- [ ] Schedule formal renewal discussion
- [ ] Send results presentation in advance
- [ ] Present renewal proposal
- [ ] Discuss future goals and strategy
- [ ] Address any objections or concerns
- [ ] Negotiate terms if needed

**Renewal Meeting Agenda**:
1. Review partnership achievements (10 min)
2. Present results and ROI (15 min)
3. Discuss future goals (10 min)
4. Present renewal proposal and next phase strategy (15 min)
5. Address questions and concerns (10 min)
6. Next steps and timeline (5 min)

### 15 Days Before Renewal
- [ ] Follow up on proposal
- [ ] Address any outstanding questions
- [ ] Send contract for signature
- [ ] Confirm start date for next phase
- [ ] Plan kickoff for renewed engagement

### 7 Days Before Renewal
- [ ] Ensure contract is signed
- [ ] If not signed, escalate urgency
- [ ] Confirm deliverables are ready for next phase
- [ ] Thank client for continued partnership

### Contract End Date
- [ ] Celebrate renewal with client
- [ ] Send thank you gift
- [ ] Ask for referral (perfect time!)
- [ ] Plan next 90 days
- [ ] Update renewal date in system for next cycle

## 🚨 Handling At-Risk Renewals

### Warning Signs a Renewal is At-Risk
- Health score below 3.5
- Declining engagement (missed meetings, slow responses)
- Budget pressure or cost complaints
- Declining performance metrics
- New decision maker who doesn't know you
- Competitor mentioned in conversations
- Defensive or negative tone in communications
- Reduction in scope requests
- Payment delays

### At-Risk Renewal Recovery Plan

**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (Within 24-48 Hours)**:
1. [ ] Schedule urgent call with decision maker
2. [ ] Prepare honest assessment of situation
3. [ ] Draft recovery plan
4. [ ] Get senior leadership involved if needed

**DURING RECOVERY CALL**:
1. Acknowledge the situation directly
2. Take ownership of any shortfalls
3. Listen deeply to their concerns
4. Ask: "What would it take to make this partnership work for you?"
5. Present recovery plan
6. Set clear milestones and check-ins
7. Get commitment to give you a chance to fix it

**RECOVERY PLAN TEMPLATE**:
- **Current Issue**: [Clear description]
- **Root Cause**: [Honest assessment]
- **Our Commitment**: [What we'll change]
- **Action Steps**: [Specific actions with dates]
- **Success Metrics**: [How we'll measure improvement]
- **Check-in Schedule**: [Weekly calls for 30 days]
- **Escalation Contact**: [Senior person they can reach]

**FOLLOW THROUGH**:
- [ ] Execute recovery plan flawlessly
- [ ] Over-communicate progress
- [ ] Weekly check-ins for 4-6 weeks
- [ ] Document all improvements
- [ ] Re-present value after 30 days

## 💰 Renewal Pricing Strategies

### Option 1: Standard Renewal (Same Price)
**When to Use**: Client is satisfied, budget is stable, market is competitive
**Positioning**: "We'd love to continue at the current rate and build on this momentum"

### Option 2: Price Increase
**When to Use**: Exceptional results, expanded scope, market rate increase
**Justification**: Document added value, expanded services, or market changes
**Increase Range**: Typically 5-15% annually
**Positioning**: "Based on [results/expanded scope], here's our recommendation..."

### Option 3: Discount (Use Sparingly)
**When to Use**: At-risk client, competitive pressure, budget constraints
**Strategy**: Tie discount to commitment (annual vs monthly) or scope adjustment
**Positioning**: "We value this partnership and want to find a way to continue"

### Option 4: Upsell Renewal
**When to Use**: Thriving client with clear additional needs
**Strategy**: Show how additional services will accelerate their results
**Positioning**: "Based on these wins, here's how we could 10x the impact..."

### Option 5: Scope Adjustment
**When to Use**: Budget pressure but still want to work together
**Strategy**: Reduce scope proportionally to reduced budget
**Positioning**: "Let's prioritize the highest-impact activities within your budget"

## 🔄 Post-Renewal Actions

### Client Renewed - Celebrate! 🎉
- [ ] Send thank you email within 24 hours
- [ ] Send thank you gift
- [ ] Ask for referral: "Who else could benefit from results like yours?"
- [ ] Request testimonial or case study
- [ ] Update client health dashboard
- [ ] Plan kickoff for next phase
- [ ] Set new renewal reminder (90 days before next renewal)
- [ ] Document what worked for this renewal
- [ ] Share win with team

### Client Did Not Renew - Learn 😔
- [ ] Conduct exit interview if possible
- [ ] Document reason for loss
- [ ] Identify what we could have done differently
- [ ] Update processes based on learnings
- [ ] Keep door open for future partnership
- [ ] Archive client file
- [ ] Update forecasts and budgets
- [ ] Team debrief on lessons learned

**Exit Interview Questions**:
- "What led to your decision not to renew?"
- "What could we have done differently?"
- "Were there any specific moments where we lost you?"
- "Would you consider working together again in the future?"
- "Can we stay in touch?"

## 💡 Renewal Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- Start renewal conversations early (90+ days)
- Document results continuously
- Build business case with data
- Address concerns proactively
- Offer strategic value beyond execution
- Make client feel like a partner
- Celebrate wins together
- Ask for feedback regularly
- Provide clear ROI
- Make renewal easy and obvious

### Don'ts ❌
- Wait until last minute
- Surprise client with price increase
- Ignore warning signs
- Take renewal for granted
- Present weak business case
- Make it only about price
- Forget to show appreciation
- Assume everything is fine
- Be defensive about issues
- Make renewal complicated

## 🎓 Renewal Conversation Scripts

### The Proactive Check-In (90 Days Out)
"Hey [Name], I wanted to touch base as we're coming up on [month] when our current agreement renews. First, how are you feeling about our partnership and the results we're driving?"

[Listen]

"Great to hear. I'd love to schedule some time in the next few weeks to review everything we've accomplished together and discuss your goals for the next phase. Does that sound good?"

### The Renewal Proposal Presentation (30 Days Out)
"Thank you for taking time for this review. I've put together a summary of what we've achieved together:

[Present results]

Based on these results and your goals for [next period], I'd like to propose continuing our partnership. Here's what I'm recommending for the next phase:

[Present proposal]

What questions do you have?"

### Handling "We Need to Reduce Budget"
"I understand budget pressures. Before we discuss pricing, can I ask - are you satisfied with the results we're delivering?"

[If yes]: "Great. Let me put together some options for how we could adjust scope to fit your budget while still delivering meaningful results. Would that be helpful?"

[If no]: "I appreciate your honesty. Let's talk about what's not working and how we can fix it."

### Asking for Referral After Renewal
"I'm so grateful for your continued trust in our partnership. Quick question - who else do you know who could benefit from results like you're seeing? I'd love to help them the way we've been able to help you."
