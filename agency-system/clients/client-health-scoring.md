# Client Health Scoring System

**Purpose**: Objectively measure client satisfaction and predict churn risk before it's too late

**Review Frequency**: Weekly minimum, daily for at-risk clients

---

## 🎯 Health Score Overview

**Overall Health Score**: Average of 5 categories (1-5 scale)
- 4.5 - 5.0 = 🟢 Thriving (Referral-ready, NPS Promoters)
- 3.5 - 4.4 = 🟡 Healthy (Satisfied, NPS Passives)
- 2.5 - 3.4 = 🟠 Needs Attention (At-risk, requires action)
- 0 - 2.4 = 🔴 Critical (High churn risk, urgent intervention needed)

---

## 📊 The 5 Health Categories

### 1. Results & Performance (Weight: 30%)

**5 - Exceptional**: Exceeding all KPIs, outstanding results
- All KPIs at 120%+ of target
- Measurable business impact documented
- Client publicly praising results

**4 - Strong**: Meeting or slightly exceeding KPIs
- KPIs at 100-120% of target
- Clear progress toward goals
- Positive results feedback

**3 - Adequate**: Meeting most KPIs, some falling short
- KPIs at 80-100% of target
- Mixed results, some wins
- Client acknowledges progress but wants more

**2 - Concerning**: Missing most KPIs
- KPIs at 50-80% of target
- Limited measurable success
- Client expressing concern about results

**1 - Critical**: Significantly underperforming
- KPIs below 50% of target
- No meaningful results to show
- Client questioning value

**How to Assess**:
- Review campaign/project performance data
- Compare actuals vs. targets
- Check recent client feedback about results
- Analyze trend (improving or declining?)

---

### 2. Engagement & Communication (Weight: 20%)

**5 - Highly Engaged**: Enthusiastic, responsive, collaborative
- Responds to emails within 24 hours
- Attends all scheduled meetings
- Proactively shares ideas and feedback
- Friendly and warm in communication

**4 - Engaged**: Responsive and participative
- Usually responds within 48 hours
- Attends most meetings
- Engages in discussions
- Professional and positive tone

**3 - Moderately Engaged**: Basic engagement
- Responds within 2-4 days
- Occasionally misses meetings
- Minimal proactive communication
- Neutral tone

**2 - Disengaged**: Slow to respond, minimal participation
- Takes 5+ days to respond
- Frequently misses meetings
- Short, curt responses
- Seems distracted or uninterested

**1 - Unresponsive**: Avoiding communication
- Rarely responds to emails
- Cancels meetings repeatedly
- Giving you the silent treatment
- Clearly pulling away

**Warning Signs**:
- Response time increasing
- Meeting attendance dropping
- Communication tone becoming cold
- Suddenly "too busy" to talk

---

### 3. Satisfaction & Sentiment (Weight: 25%)

**5 - Thrilled**: Actively praising your work
- Unsolicited positive feedback
- Introducing you to colleagues
- Would score 9-10 on NPS
- Excited about working together

**4 - Satisfied**: Happy with the partnership
- Positive feedback when asked
- Would score 7-8 on NPS
- Comfortable continuing relationship

**3 - Neutral**: Neither positive nor negative
- Minimal feedback
- Would score 5-6 on NPS
- Transactional relationship
- "It's fine" attitude

**2 - Dissatisfied**: Expressing concerns
- Complaints or criticism
- Would score 3-4 on NPS
- Frustration evident
- Questioning decisions

**1 - Very Unhappy**: Actively upset
- Angry or disappointed
- Would score 0-2 on NPS
- Threatening to leave
- Hostile communication

**How to Assess**:
- Recent NPS or satisfaction survey
- Tone of recent communications
- Feedback in meetings
- Complaints or compliments
- Body language and energy in calls

---

### 4. Financial Health (Weight: 15%)

**5 - Excellent**: Growing, no payment issues
- Pays on time or early
- Has increased budget/scope
- Discussing additional services
- No billing disputes

**4 - Good**: Stable and reliable
- Pays on time consistently
- Budget stable
- No payment concerns

**3 - Acceptable**: Occasional issues
- Occasional late payment (within 15 days)
- Budget pressure mentioned
- Scrutinizing invoices closely

**2 - Concerning**: Payment problems
- Frequently late (15-30 days)
- Requested budget reduction
- Disputed invoices
- Cash flow issues evident

**1 - Critical**: Serious financial problems
- 30+ days overdue
- Not responding to invoices
- Announced budget cuts
- Payment plans needed

**Red Flags**:
- Payment delays increasing
- Asking for discounts suddenly
- Scrutinizing every line item
- Requesting scope reductions

---

### 5. Strategic Alignment & Value (Weight: 10%)

**5 - Perfect Fit**: You're essential to their strategy
- They rely on your expertise
- Involve you in strategic planning
- See you as trusted advisor
- Long-term vision together

**4 - Strategic Partner**: Valued contributor
- Regular strategy discussions
- Trust your recommendations
- Think long-term
- See you as more than vendor

**3 - Valued Vendor**: Transactional but valued
- Execute what they ask
- Limited strategic input
- Month-to-month thinking
- Replaceable but doing good work

**2 - Commodity Provider**: Just another vendor
- No strategic discussions
- Price-focused conversations
- Short-term thinking
- Easy to replace

**1 - Misalignment**: Not a good fit
- Fundamental disagreements
- Different values or priorities
- You're not the right solution
- Should probably part ways

## 🚨 Automated Warning Triggers

Set up alerts when:

### 🟠 Yellow Alert - Needs Attention
- Overall score drops below 3.5
- Any category scores 2 or below
- Score declined 0.5+ points in a month
- Payment is 10+ days late
- Client missed 2 consecutive meetings

**Action Required**:
- Schedule check-in call within 1 week
- Address specific concerns
- Create improvement plan

### 🔴 Red Alert - Critical
- Overall score drops below 2.5
- Any category scores 1
- Score declined 1.0+ points in a month
- Payment is 30+ days late
- Client not responding to communication

**Action Required**:
- Emergency call within 24-48 hours
- Senior leadership involvement
- Recovery plan implementation
- Daily monitoring

## 📅 Scoring Schedule

### Weekly Quick Check (Monday)
- Review any significant client interactions from last week
- Update scores if major changes
- Flag anything requiring attention

### Monthly Deep Assessment
- Complete full health scoring for all clients
- Document trends
- Update action plans
- Report to team

### Quarterly Comprehensive Review
- Full assessment with client interviews/surveys
- NPS survey
- Strategy alignment discussion
- Long-term outlook evaluation

## 🎯 Using Health Scores

### For Thriving Clients (4.5 - 5.0) 🟢
**Actions**:
- Ask for referrals
- Request testimonials
- Develop case studies
- Explore upsell opportunities
- Celebrate partnership
- Feature in marketing
- Maintain excellence

### For Healthy Clients (3.5 - 4.4) 🟡
**Actions**:
- Continue good work
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Strengthen specific weak areas
- Deepen relationship
- Plan for elevation to thriving

### For Clients Needing Attention (2.5 - 3.4) 🟠
**Actions**:
- Schedule intervention call
- Create improvement plan
- Address specific issues
- Increase communication frequency
- Deliver quick wins
- Weekly monitoring

### For Critical Clients (0 - 2.4) 🔴
**Actions**:
- Emergency meeting
- Senior leadership involvement
- Recovery plan implementation
- Consider if partnership should continue
- Daily check-ins
- Document everything

## 💡 Improving Health Scores

### To Improve Results (Category 1)
- Audit current campaigns/strategies
- Implement optimizations immediately
- Set more achievable short-term goals
- Celebrate small wins
- Over-communicate results

### To Improve Engagement (Category 2)
- Increase proactive outreach
- Make meetings more valuable
- Respond faster to their messages
- Ask better questions
- Be more available

### To Improve Satisfaction (Category 3)
- Conduct feedback session
- Address pain points
- Deliver unexpected value
- Show you care personally
- Fix any frustrations

### To Improve Financial (Category 4)
- Have honest budget conversation
- Offer flexible payment if needed
- Justify pricing with value
- Simplify billing
- Be understanding of their situation

### To Improve Strategic Alignment (Category 5)
- Provide strategic insights
- Think bigger picture
- Recommend beyond scope
- Challenge their thinking
- Become advisor, not just vendor
