## 🎯 Referral Program Philosophy

**Key Principle**: People refer businesses they trust to help people they care about.

**The Math**: If you have 10 thriving clients and each refers 1 qualified prospect per year, that's 10 new opportunities. If you close 50%, that's 5 new clients without any marketing spend.

**When to Ask**: After wins, during renewals, when client is clearly thrilled with results.

**Who to Ask**: Only clients with health scores of 4.5+ who are seeing real results.

## 🗣️ How to Ask for Referrals

### The Perfect Moment

**BEST TIMES TO ASK**:
1. **After a Major Win**: "We just increased your leads by 300%!"
2. **During Renewal**: "We're thrilled to continue this partnership"
3. **After Unsolicited Praise**: "So glad to hear you're happy!"
4. **Quarterly Business Review**: After showing strong results
5. **Anniversary**: "We've been working together for a year!"

**DON'T ASK WHEN**:
- Client is frustrated
- Results are declining
- You've recently made a mistake
- Client is distracted or stressed
- You don't have a strong relationship yet

### Referral Ask Scripts

**Script 1: The Direct Ask (After Win)**
```
"[Client], I'm so thrilled with these results we're seeing together. Quick question - who else do you know in your network who could benefit from results like these? I'd love to help them the way we've been able to help you."
```

**Script 2: The Specific Ask**
```
"[Client], I'm looking to work with 2-3 more companies in the [industry] space. Based on our work together, who comes to mind that might be a good fit and could use help with [their problem]?"
```

**Script 3: The Grateful Ask (After Renewal)**
```
"Thank you so much for renewing our partnership. Your trust means everything to us. If you know anyone who could benefit from the kind of work we do together, I'd be honored if you'd introduce us."
```

**Script 4: The Value-First Ask**
```
"I've been thinking about [Contact's Company] and noticed they could probably benefit from [specific solution]. Would you be comfortable making an introduction? I'd love to help them like we've helped you."
```

**Script 5: The Casual Ask**
```
"By the way, who's the best [their role] you know? I love working with folks like you and am looking to grow the business with more great clients."
```

### How to Handle Responses

**"Yes, I know someone!"**
- Get details: name, company, best way to connect
- Ask about relationship: "How do you know them?"
- Ask for warm intro: "Would you be comfortable introducing us via email?"
- Provide easy template they can use
- Follow up within 24 hours
- Keep them updated on progress

**"Let me think about it"**
- "No pressure at all! If someone comes to mind, just let me know."
- Follow up in 2 weeks
- Make it easy: "Even just a name would be helpful"
- Don't be pushy

**"Not right now"**
- "No problem at all! If anyone comes to mind in the future, I'd appreciate it."
- Don't take it personally
- Focus on continuing to deliver great work
- Ask again in 6 months

## 🎓 Referral Best Practices

### DO ✅
- Ask clients who are thrilled
- Make it easy for them
- Provide intro templates
- Follow up quickly
- Keep them updated
- Reward generously
- Say thank you multiple times
- Make referrals a regular part of client conversations

### DON'T ❌
- Ask clients who aren't happy
- Be pushy or desperate
- Forget to follow up
- Take referrals for granted
- Ignore the referring client after you get the name
- Forget to pay incentive
- Make it complicated
- Only ask once and give up
