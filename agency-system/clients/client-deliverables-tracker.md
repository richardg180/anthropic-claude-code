## 📞 Client Communication About Deliverables

### When Deliverable Will Be Late

**DO THIS**:
1. Notify client ASAP (don't wait until due date)
2. Take ownership (no excuses)
3. Explain what happened
4. Provide new timeline
5. Explain what you're doing to prevent future delays
6. Offer something extra to make up for it

**EMAIL TEMPLATE**:
```
Subject: [Deliverable] Update

Hi [Client Name],

I wanted to reach out proactively about [deliverable] that was due [date].

Due to [brief, honest reason], we're going to need until [new date] to deliver this at the quality level you deserve. I take full responsibility for this delay.

Here's what we're doing:
- [Action 1]
- [Action 2]

To make up for this, I'd like to [offer something extra - bonus analysis, additional deliverable, etc.].

I'll send an update tomorrow at [time], and you'll have the completed [deliverable] by [date].

I apologize for this delay and appreciate your understanding.

[Your name]
```

### Proactive Delivery Updates

**BEST PRACTICE**: Send status updates before client asks

**MID-WEEK CHECK-IN**:
"Quick update: [Deliverable] is on track for [date]. I'll have a draft to you by [earlier date] if you'd like to preview."

**EARLY DELIVERY**:
"Great news - [deliverable] is ready a day early! Here it is: [link]"
