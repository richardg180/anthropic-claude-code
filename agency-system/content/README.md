# Content Operations System

Manage all content production, planning, and publishing from this centralized hub. Stay organized and never miss a deadline.

## 📋 Overview

- **Content Calendar**: Master calendar in `content-calendar.md`
- **Content Briefs**: Individual briefs for each piece
- **Editorial Guidelines**: Standards and best practices
- **Content Library**: Archive of published content

## 🎯 Content Types Managed

- Whitepapers and ebooks
- Case studies
- Press releases

### Visual Content

- Infographics

### Video Content
- YouTube videos
- Social media videos
- Webinars

- Testimonials

### Interactive Content
- Quizzes and assessments
- Calculators
- Interactive infographics
- Webinars and events

## 📊 Content Workflow

### 1. Ideation & Planning
- Brainstorm content ideas
- Align with client goals and campaigns
- Research keywords and topics
- Add to content calendar

### 2. Creation
- Create content brief
- Assign to writer/creator
- Draft content
- Include SEO optimization
- Review internally

### 3. Review & Approval
- Client review (if needed)
- Revisions
- Final approval
- Legal/compliance check (if needed)

### 4. Publishing
- Schedule in publishing platform
- Publish content
- Share on appropriate channels
- Update calendar with live links

### 5. Promotion
- Share on social media
- Include in email newsletters
- Paid promotion (if planned)
- Track performance

### 6. Performance Tracking
- Monitor metrics
- Document learnings
- Update content strategy

## 🎯 Best Practices

1. **Plan Ahead** - Minimum 2 weeks in advance
2. **Batch Creation** - More efficient than one-off pieces
3. **Repurpose Content** - One piece, multiple formats
4. **SEO First** - Always optimize for search
5. **Quality Over Quantity** - Better to publish less but better
6. **Track Everything** - Monitor what works
7. **Stay On Brand** - Consistent voice and style
