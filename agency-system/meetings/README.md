# Meeting Management System

Run productive meetings and maintain clear communication with clients and your team.

## 📋 Overview

Effective meetings are essential for agency success. Use these templates to ensure every meeting has a clear purpose, agenda, and actionable outcomes.

## 🎯 Meeting Types

### Client Meetings
- Kickoff meetings
- Weekly/bi-weekly check-ins
- Monthly business reviews
- Quarterly strategic reviews
- Campaign reviews
- Crisis meetings

### Internal Meetings
- Daily standups
- Weekly team meetings
- Project planning sessions
- Post-mortems/retrospectives
- One-on-ones
- All-hands meetings

## 🎯 Meeting Best Practices

### Before the Meeting
1. **Set Clear Objective** - Why are we meeting?
2. **Create Agenda** - What will we cover?
3. **Share in Advance** - Give attendees time to prepare
4. **Time Box Topics** - Allocate time for each item
5. **Invite Right People** - Only those who need to be there

### During the Meeting
1. **Start on Time** - Respect everyone's schedule
2. **Follow the Agenda** - Stay on track
3. **Take Notes** - Capture decisions and action items
4. **Encourage Participation** - Get input from everyone
5. **End with Clear Next Steps** - Everyone knows what to do

### After the Meeting
1. **Send Notes Promptly** - Within 24 hours
2. **List Action Items** - With owners and due dates
3. **Follow Up** - Track completion of action items
4. **File Notes** - Store in appropriate client/project folder
5. **Review Effectiveness** - Was the meeting valuable?

## 📊 Meeting Metrics to Track

- Meeting productivity (achieved objectives?)
- Action item completion rate
- Time spent in meetings per week
- Client satisfaction with meetings
